### Project Brief

i am building a website that allow user to upload pictures of exotic animal, place, people, cars,etc.
the user can share these image with family and friend.

### 🎯 Your Custom Color Palette

Here’s a strong starting palette for your idea:

- Background (Primary) → #0F172A (deep navy/near black)
- Surface (Cards/UI panels) → #1E293B
- Primary Accent (main action) → #22C55E (vibrant green)
- Secondary Accent → #3B82F6 (blue for links/interactions)
- Highlight/Explore vibe → #F97316 (orange for discovery/exotic feel)
- Text (main) → #F8FAFC (almost white)
- Text (secondary) → #94A3B8

### 🧠 Why This Works

- Dark background → lets colorful photos stand out
- Green (#22C55E) → feels fresh, “upload/share/positive action”
- Blue (#3B82F6) → trust + familiar UI interactions
- Orange (#F97316) → adds that “explore something exotic” energy
- Neutral grays → keep everything readable without distraction

### 🖥️ How You’d Use These

- Upload button → Green (primary action)
- Links / clickable elements → Blue
- “Explore” or trending tags → Orange
- Background → Dark navy
- Cards (image containers) → Slightly lighter dark
